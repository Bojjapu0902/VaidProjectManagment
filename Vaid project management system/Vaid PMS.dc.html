01

Assumptions & the model I designed against

Assumed

The drawing is the unit of work

Tasks, approvals, RFIs and site snags all hang off a numbered sheet and its revision. Nothing exists loose. The register is the single source of truth for "what is the latest".

Assumed

CAD files stay on the local server

A watched folder per project. The app stores the record, a flattened PDF/PNG for viewing and markup, and a link back to the DWG/RVT path. Nobody re-uploads by hand.

Assumed

Clients see a walled garden

The portal shows milestones, issued sets and pending approvals only — never internal tasks, hours or fee data. Same data, separate shell.

Placeholder phase model — replace with Vaid's real naming
01 Brief & Feasibility → 02 Concept → 03 Design Development → 04 Working Drawings / GFC → 05 Tender & Award → 06 Site Execution → 07 Handover & Snags

Phases are a per-project template, not hardcoded — an interior fit-out skips tender, a masterplan adds statutory approvals. Every phase carries a fee %, a date range, a deliverable set and a client approval gate.

Ink on paper, hairline rules, no rounded cards. Colour is reserved for status: red revise, green approved, amber in review, blue issued. Everything else is monochrome — like a drawing sheet.
